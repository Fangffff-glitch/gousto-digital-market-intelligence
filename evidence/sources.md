## Data dictionary

- `competitor-snapshot.csv`: rounded vendor estimates transcribed from S01; traffic is not customer count; branded shares are percentages; date is the displayed report date.
- `keyword-candidates.csv`: written-coursework values from S03, with explicitly new priority judgements.
- `evidence-register.csv`: claim-level provenance, interpretation, evidence strength, missing data and revision treatment. An AI field is populated only where an original disclosure supports it; other rows are not invented AI outputs.
