# Market intelligence review

This page develops my original MSIN0308 sourcing, framing and integration work. The judgements and proposed decision rules below were revised on **6 November 2026**; they are not presented as the original group decisions or as a new collection of customer evidence.

## Decision context

The investigation asks what evidence would help distinguish sustainable acquisition from promotion dependency, and whether customer-experience signals require changes to priorities. Its intended audience is commercial and operational leadership.

Public signals can identify questions that management should investigate. Internal data are needed to establish their frequency, financial significance and cause.

## A source system with explicit limits

| Signal family | Sources used in my original research | Contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market context | Kantar and competitor/industry materials | Context for consumer budgets and positioning questions | Aggregate conditions do not measure Gousto-specific price elasticity. |
| Acquisition messages | Meta Ad Library and Reddit discussions | Visibility of promotional messages and reported deal-seeking | Ad visibility is not conversion, spend, programme coverage or cohort profitability. |
| Customer experience | Trustpilot and other review/discussion sources cited in the project | Qualitative leads about customer friction | Self-selected reports do not establish population prevalence or verified incidents. |
| Technology and operations | Company/technology narratives and Lens.org | Questions about claimed capabilities and possible technical developments | Publicity is not retention evidence; a patent is not proof of deployment or failure. |

The retained report describes these activities, but exact ad IDs, review IDs, sampling windows and a complete raw archive were not recovered. The [source register](../evidence/sources.md) therefore marks these as report-level provenance, rather than fully reproduced primary evidence.

## Three questions that change the decision

### 1. Does promotion activity imply unprofitable acquisition?

**Retained record:** my individual AI disclosure describes comparing a positive loyalty interpretation with Meta advertising and Reddit discussions about discounts (E07–E08).

**Interpretation:** promotion-sensitive behaviour is a plausible research hypothesis. Visible discounts can also be a normal acquisition mechanism, and discussion participants may be unusually deal-focused. Positive reviews and discount use can coexist.

**Revised judgement:** neither loyal profitable cohorts nor zero-LTV cohorts are established by this evidence. Traffic and ad activity do not show who converted, retained or generated contribution margin.

**Decision:** request retention, net revenue, variable fulfilment costs, refunds, promotion costs and acquisition spend by acquisition cohort. If testing offers, compare like-for-like eligible populations against an appropriate control. No universal discount termination or specific replacement percentage is justified by the public record alone.

**What would change the conclusion?** Discount-acquired cohorts with sustained positive contribution and acceptable payback would weaken the dependency concern; repeated contribution losses relative to comparable cohorts would strengthen it.

### 2. Does competitor positioning determine Gousto's pricing choice?

**Retained record:** the research uses competitor and macro sources to frame a pricing tension (E09). The SEO audit separately shows differences in search reach and mix.

**Interpretation:** competitor positioning can identify options. Aggregate inflation and search visibility cannot determine Gousto customers' willingness to pay or the profitability of a specific price change.

**Revised judgement:** there is insufficient evidence for a blanket instruction to adopt or reject a premium strategy. Segments may value convenience, menu choice and price differently.

**Decision:** compare matched customer propositions and total basket costs; test clearly explained value messages or offers in defined segments. Record preparation time and product differences when comparing recipe boxes with prepared meals or supermarket ingredients.

**What would change the conclusion?** Stable conversion and retention with improved contribution in a defined segment would support expansion; weakening retention or contribution would argue for adjustment.

### 3. Do customer complaints and patents establish an operational cause?

**Retained record:** the original sourcing combined review themes with patent material (E10–E11).

**Interpretation:** complaints can prompt an incident investigation. The same theme appearing in several places may reflect related or duplicated accounts rather than independent corroboration. Patent publication establishes neither Gousto's deployment of a design nor the cause of a complaint.

**Revised judgement:** withdraw the claimed causal link between a packaging patent and operational failure. No regulatory event, systemic failure rate or AI inventory-clearing mechanism is established here.

**Decision:** validate incidents using support records and delivery/product traces, assess severity and route confirmed issues to the responsible operational team. Evaluate app work and operational work against their actual dependencies and resources; a blanket app-investment freeze is not supported.

**What would change the conclusion?** Verified incident records, representative rates and a deployment timeline could support a narrower operational conclusion. Resolution evidence and stable rates would reduce concern.

## Responsible interpretation

My original integration work considered bias, privacy and who might be disadvantaged by commercial decisions. This revision makes those concerns operational:

- Aggregate public discussion themes without republishing handles or personal narratives.
- Record source selection, missing satisfied/non-posting customers and possible duplicates.
- Do not infer income or vulnerability from a username or a complaint; assess affordability implications through suitable aggregated research.
- Keep a record of rejected and unresolved claims, including human overinterpretation.

The portfolio does not describe an unverified scraping process as a reproducible dataset. It also does not retain numerical ethics scores whose calibration is undocumented.

The result is a set of justified investigations and bounded tests. The [action plan](../recommendations/action-plan.md) is a new extension; other members' individual decision and pre-mortem sections are not reproduced as my work.
