## Evidence labels

**Screenshot-checked:** the reported value was checked against a retained original screenshot during this revision. This confirms transcription, not the accuracy of the underlying third-party estimate.

**Coursework-reported:** present in the original written analysis, but the precise supporting export or source item was not independently recovered.

**Disclosure-reported:** the original individual disclosure describes a process or interpretation; a full prompt/output transcript is unavailable.

**Portfolio extension:** new synthesis, corrective interpretation or proposed validation, dated separately from the original research.

Confidence in a source reading is distinct from confidence in a business inference. A clearly legible traffic number can still provide weak evidence about profitability.

## What is reproducible

Readers can inspect the transcribed comparison and keyword tables, recompute the stated arithmetic, follow evidence IDs and see why conclusions were narrowed. This repository does not contain the complete historical SEMrush export or a coded review dataset. Therefore the original vendor estimates and consumer-theme prevalence cannot be independently reproduced from this repository alone.

No internal client data, live interventions, model evaluation metrics or implemented business outcomes are claimed.
