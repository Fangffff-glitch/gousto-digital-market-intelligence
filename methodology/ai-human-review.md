# AI and human judgement

## A documented individual example

The MSIN0308 individual disclosure associated with my contribution records Gemini being used to scan and summarise acquisition, positioning and consumer sentiment. It describes an AI interpretation of strong loyalty driven by personalisation and digital marketing, followed by my challenge using advertising and consumer-discussion sources.

This is a **paraphrase of a retained disclosure**, not a verbatim model transcript. The original prompts, full outputs and an item-level archive of the cited ads/posts were not recovered for this portfolio. No replacement dialogue has been invented.

| Stage | What the retained record supports | Revised assessment |
|---|---|---|
| AI interpretation | The disclosure reports an optimistic loyalty/acquisition interpretation. | Public technology and marketing narratives do not establish retained behaviour or profitability. |
| My original challenge | I compared that account with Meta advertising and Reddit promotion discussions. | Those sources can challenge confidence in the interpretation and suggest competing explanations. |
| My original conclusion | The disclosure went further and described customers as zero-LTV and entirely subsidised. | This was also unsupported: ads and discussion cannot quantify cohort lifetime value or its prevalence. |
| Portfolio correction, 6 November 2026 | Both the optimistic interpretation and my categorical negative conclusion are narrowed. | Promotion dependency remains a hypothesis requiring cohort retention and contribution data. |
| Decision consequence | Move away from a categorical commercial mandate. | Request internal evidence and design a controlled, segment-specific test before changing offers broadly. |

The lesson is not that human disagreement validates a conclusion. **A correction needs stronger evidence than the claim it replaces.** My original challenge identified a useful blind spot; this revision also subjects that challenge to scrutiny.

## Evidence needed for the next iteration

For each future AI-assisted claim, retain the prompt, model/date, relevant output, exact source item and date, alternative explanation, confidence judgement and resulting decision change. Preserve only necessary public-source excerpts and avoid personal identifiers.

A useful outcome may be “unresolved”. Two competing narratives can both exceed the available evidence.
